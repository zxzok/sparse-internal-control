One push is a demo. Control means you can hit a target, undo it, dial it, track it, spare everything else, and reuse it on prompts you have never seen. Each axis below is one of those requirements, made measurable.

00 / Setup

What every axis needs.

The axes apply to any intervention that has a support, base strengths and a direction rule. Nothing in the protocol depends on how the sites were chosen.

01 / Intervention

Support and strengths

A support \(S\) of internal sites, base strengths \(\beta\), and a rule for the direction added at each site.

02 / Dose

One scale

A scalar dose \(\gamma\) multiplies the strengths. \(\gamma = 1\) is the nominal intervention and \(\gamma = 0\) is the unpatched model.

03 / Readouts

Source, target, patched

A task readout on the source input, on the target input, and on the source input under the patch.

\(y_{\mathrm{src}},\ \ y_{\mathrm{tar}},\ \ y_{\mathrm{pat}}\)

04 / Panels

Fit and evaluate

Fitting and evaluation prompts are split, seeds are repeated, and off-target prompts come from a separate fixed panel.

P and F

Two direction conditions run side by side. P uses evaluation-pair activation contrasts; F uses training-mean contrasts from the fitting split. The direction rule is independent of coefficient fitting and of dose updates.

Scope

Thresholds are operating choices, and decisions use unrounded values. The six axes characterize the control an intervention provides; on their own they do not establish the joint \((\varepsilon, \delta, \kappa)\) certificate, and the reported results do not claim it was evaluated.

01 / hit

Reachability

How close does the intervention get to the target?

Probe
Apply the intervention at its nominal dose \(\gamma = 1\) and read the target behavior on evaluation prompts the selector did not fit (the same panel for IOI).
Statistic
\(\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{tar}}(1) = \dfrac{\lVert y_{\mathrm{tar}} - y_{\mathrm{pat}} \rVert_2}{\lVert y_{\mathrm{tar}} - y_{\mathrm{src}} \rVert_2 + \xi}\), computed on each complete readout vector before averaging across seeds.
Passes when
\(\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{tar}}(1) \le \varepsilon = 0.4\). The appendix repeats the count for \(\varepsilon\) from 0.3 to 0.5.
Reported as
Passing seeds, with the mean and sample standard deviation.

02 / undo

Signed reversal

Does the opposite patch move target-side behavior toward the source?

Probe
Apply the opposite patch \(-\beta\) to the target-side input. For Refusal, the negative coefficients act on the harmful member of each pair.
Statistic
\(\mathrm{Reversal}(\beta) = \lVert y_{-\beta} - y_{\mathrm{tar}} \rVert_2 \,/\, \lVert y_{-\beta} - y_{\mathrm{src}} \rVert_2\): the distance of the reversed output from the target over its distance from the source. Infinite and 0/0 ratios are kept explicit.
Passes when
\(\mathrm{Reversal}(\beta) \ge 2\): the reversed output ends at least twice as close to the source as to the target.
Reported as
Passing seeds, with the mean ratio and standard deviation.

03 / dial

Dose response

How does target progress vary with intervention strength?

Probe
Hold the support and base strengths fixed and scale them by ten doses \(\gamma \in \{-1, -0.5, 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2\}\).
Statistic
Target progress \(P(\gamma)\) at each dose and the signed Spearman \(\rho\) of progress against \(\gamma\). Ties use average ranks; a constant curve is undefined.
Passes when
\(\rho \ge 0.95\). A strong negative correlation is reported separately and never counts as ordered.
Reported as
Curves with \(\rho \ge 0.95\) and with \(\rho \le -0.95\), out of 8 seeds.

04 / track

Feedback controllability

Can feedback-adjusted doses track requested targets?

Probe
Keep the support, base strengths and direction rule fixed, request a target value \(m^\star\), and update only the dose from the measured error: \(\gamma_{t+1} = \gamma_t + \eta\,(m^\star - M(\gamma_t))\), with a fixed gain.
Statistic
The terminal error \(\lvert m^\star - M(\gamma_{\mathrm{end}}) \rvert\) per endpoint, with hits, mean absolute error and the number of readout evaluations. Targets already met at zero dose are reported separately.
Passes when
\(\lvert m^\star - M(\gamma_{\mathrm{end}}) \rvert \le \tau_{\mathrm{ctrl}}\): 0.3 for IOI and MCQA, 0.1 for Refusal.
Reported as
Hits over endpoints: 8 seeds × 6 targets for MCQA, 8 × 5 for Refusal, 3 × 8 for IOI.

05 / spare

Off-target preservation

How much do non-target outputs change?

Probe
Apply the intervention at its nominal dose to a fixed panel of 32 unrelated MMLU prompts (16 Humanities, 16 STEM): 256 prompt–seed observations per selector.
Statistic
Full-vocabulary \(\mathrm{KL}\big(p_\theta \,\Vert\, p_\theta^{\beta}\big)\) in nats, unpatched against patched, summarized by mean, median, nearest-rank P95 and maximum.
Passes when
\(\mathrm{KL} \le \delta = 0.001\), counted twice: on the seed mean, and on every prompt of the seed.
Reported as
Passing seeds under each rule, with the KL distribution.

06 / reuse

Held-out reuse

Do selected sites or complete interventions transfer to unseen prompts?

Probe
Carry the support selected on fitting prompts to unseen prompts, either refitting the strengths there (refit) or freezing the whole intervention, dose included (frozen).
Statistic
Recovery of the source-to-target gap on the unseen prompts: signed mean-margin recovery for MCQA, with task-specific variants for Sentiment and IOI. Recovery is not \(1 - \mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{tar}}\).
Passes when
No threshold. Recovery is reported as a continuous value together with its information condition.
Reported as
Mean recovery and standard deviation for each condition.
Code

08 / Use the protocol

Score your own intervention.

Activation steering, sparse autoencoder features, concept erasure or circuit discovery: any intervention with a support, strengths and a direction rule can be scored the same way. Each axis is one function in the released package.

# readouts are plain lists of floats
from sparse_control.metrics.reachability import rel_tar
from sparse_control.metrics.reversal import reversal_metrics
from sparse_control.metrics.dose import signed_spearman
from sparse_control.metrics.feedback import endpoint_metrics
from sparse_control.metrics.preservation import preservation_summary
from sparse_control.metrics.reuse import mcqa_recovery

rel_tar(source, target, patched) # reach: <= 0.4
reversal_metrics(source, target, reversed_out)["passes"] # ratio >= 2
signed_spearman(doses, progress) # dial: >= 0.95
endpoint_metrics(target=m_star, terminal_margin=m_end,
                 initial_margin=m_0, tolerance=0.3)["hit"]
preservation_summary(kl_values, seed_ids) # delta = 0.001
mcqa_recovery(source, target, patched) # reuse
