03 / Control evaluation

Six axes.
A common protocol.

Beyond target movement.

  1. \(\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{tar}}(1) = \dfrac{\|q - F(\beta)\|_2}{\|q\|_2 + \xi}\)

    Passes when\(\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{tar}}(1) \le \varepsilon = 0.4\) on held out readouts.

    In the paperCoverage-Driver passes 8/8 seeds with evaluation-pair directions and 0/8 with training-mean directions on MCQA and Sentiment.

    Source → target

    The distance left to the target is measured.

    Nominal dose
  2. \(\mathrm{Reversal}(\beta) = \dfrac{\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{dis}\to\mathrm{clean}}}{\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{dis}\to\mathrm{src}}}\)

    Passes when\(\mathrm{Reversal}(\beta) \ge 2\) after applying \(-\beta\) to the target-side input.

    In the paperMCQA passes 21/24 (Humanities) and 23/24 (STEM) cells with evaluation-pair directions and none with training-mean directions. Refusal never passes.

    Start from the target side

    Opposite patch, target-side input.

    Target-side input
  3. \(P(\gamma) = \dfrac{\langle q, F(\gamma\beta)\rangle}{\|q\|_2^2 + \xi}\)

    Passes whenSigned Spearman \(\rho \ge 0.95\) over ten doses \(\gamma \in \Gamma\).

    In the paperAll 48 Refusal curves have \(\rho = 1\), yet none passes signed reversal.

    Support fixed, dose varies

    Same sites at every dose; only the scale changes.

    γ 0.80
  4. \(\gamma_{t+1} = \gamma_t + \eta\,\big(m^{\star} - M(\gamma_t)\big)\)

    Passes when\(|m^{\star} - M(\gamma_{\mathrm{end}})| \le \tau_{\mathrm{ctrl}}\): 0.3 for IOI and MCQA, 0.1 for Refusal.

    In the paperCoverage-Driver hits 46/48 Humanities and 47/48 STEM targets with evaluation-pair directions, and 8/48 and 7/48 with training-mean directions.

    Target error drives the dose

    Only γ changes between steps.

    Update 0
  5. \(L_{\mathrm{off}}(\beta) = \tfrac{1}{n_{\mathrm{off}}}\textstyle\sum_j \mathrm{KL}\big(p_\theta(\cdot\,|\,\tilde x_j)\,\|\,p_\theta^{\beta}(\cdot\,|\,\tilde x_j)\big)\)

    Passes when\(\mathrm{KL} \le \delta = 0.001\), as a seed mean and on every prompt of a shared 32 prompt panel.

    In the paperNo MCQA or Sentiment seed passes. On Refusal, Driver-OMP passes 4/8 seeds by the mean rule.

    Non-target prompts

    ■ Baseline   ■ Patched

    Same dose
  6. \(\mathrm{recovery} = \dfrac{\bar b_{\mathrm{patched}} - \bar b_{\mathrm{source}}}{\bar b_{\mathrm{target}} - \bar b_{\mathrm{source}}}\)

    Reported asContinuous recovery with its information condition: refit-\(\beta\) or fully frozen-\(\beta\).

    In the paperModule-level MCQA recovers 0.992 with refit strengths (Humanities) and 0.021 when the whole intervention is frozen.

    Training sites, unseen prompts

    Reuse the sites and refit, or freeze the whole patch.
